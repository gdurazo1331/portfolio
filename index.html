Developed a MATLAB-based six-degree-of-freedom simulation to predict rocket apogee using thrust curves, aerodynamic drag, and dynamic mass depletion. The model integrates motor data and OpenRocket aerodynamic coefficients to generate altitude predictions.

Level 1 Rocket Project

Designed, assembled, and launched a Level 1 high-power rocket certified through the Illinois Institute of Technology Rocketry Team. This project involved stability analysis, center-of-pressure calculations, fin geometry optimization, and recovery system design. The successful flight demonstrated aerodynamic stability and safe recovery.

IREC Competition Rocket

Open PDF in a new tab

Currently working with the Illinois Tech Rocketry Team to design and simulate a rocket for the International Rocketry Engineering Competition (IREC). My duties on the structure sub team is to collaborate with the design using CAD modeling and using ANSYS to perform structural analysis to obtain \ data for the apogee calculator that is being created. The end goal is to create a rocket that reaches the target altitude of 10,000 feet, carrying a payload, and predicting the apogee of the rocket using the apogee calculator. The OpenRocket model and design documentation are shown alongside the project details.

Future Project: Lunar Transportation & Maintenance

My team and I are hoping to participate in a NASA RASC-AL competition focused on developing transportation infrastructure for the Moon. Our proposed research will explore how lunar mobility systems can support surface operations, remain maintainable in a harsh environment, and contribute to longer-term exploration and resource utilization.
